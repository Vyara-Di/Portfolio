01 — Overview

Wine labels usually compete by shouting. Reverto asked for the opposite: one serif mark, a palette from soil and glass, and labels that read as typographic studies first.

Reverto Winery — 01+
Reverto Winery — 02+
02 — Approach

The brief was blunt: hold your own on a shelf built for tradition, without its usual tricks. So the system leans on proportion and paper stock, with a fixed grid for varietal information and a palette that shifts slightly by vintage.

Restraint isn't the absence of a decision — on this shelf, it was the boldest one available.

04 — Outcome
