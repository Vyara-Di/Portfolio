02 — The Account

Working a single client at agency scale is a different discipline than moving between accounts. There's no starting over each brief — instead, the job is holding one system steady across dozens of unrelated deliverables, each with its own stakeholders, timeline, and market context, while everything still has to read as the same brand.

That means the work here isn't one narrative arc. It's a portfolio inside a portfolio — a running list of what the account actually needed, month over month.

Not one campaign — a full-year rhythm of work that all has to read as the same brand.

04 — Reflection

Seen together, none of this reads as a single "project" in the usual portfolio sense — and that's the point. The value of a long single-client engagement isn't one hero piece, it's the discipline of keeping a system coherent across a year of unrelated asks, so the brand never feels like it's being reinvented brief to brief.
